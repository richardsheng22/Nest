# Nest

A cozy, one-thumb puzzle game for iPhone. Erwu the cat is already asleep in the middle of an unfinished nest. Drag in scraps of fabric, knitting, cushions, clover, lavender and twigs to build it round her, layer by layer, one night at a time.

## Play

Open the GitHub Pages site on your iPhone in Safari, tap Share, then **Add to Home Screen**. It then opens full-screen like an app, and your best score, current night and keepsakes are saved on the phone.

## How it plays

- **Place pieces:** drag a piece from the basket into the nest. Fill a whole ring to weave it and score.
- **Layers:** weave enough rings and the outermost ring becomes a permanent layer of the nest, stitched in with a needle. Each layer makes the nest smaller, down to the lining, which finishes the night.
- **Erwu's wish:** each layer she dreams of one material. Weave a ring with enough of it to make the wish come true.
- **Tonight's goals:** three small goals each night for bonus points.
- **Themed nights:** each night brings its own mix of materials, cushion and rim.
- **Treasures:** some pieces carry a ribbon, button, felt heart, patch, lace bow or ladybird. They go into the keepsake tin, and a full set of four colours decorates every nest.
- **The pincushion:** drop a piece on it to keep it for later.
- **Erwu's help:** tap Erwu for new pieces once the ring around her has filled.

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole game (HTML, CSS and canvas JavaScript, no build step)
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `icons/`: Home Screen install
- `.nojekyll`: tells GitHub Pages to serve the files as they are

The design notes and feedback log live in the `nest/` folder of the `idea-sketches` repo.
